Features

Everything you need on stage

It feels like studio hardware: big buttons, LEDs and a display you can read from across the stage.

Setlists with real timings

Build setlists from your songs, see exactly how long each one runs (tempo changes included) and jump to the next with one tap.

Stage view

The current section in huge letters, what's next and the bars left. Open it on every musician's iPad, view-only.

Jumps that land on time

Change section or song and the jump lands on the next bar, even with a 5/4 bar inside a 4/4 song. With a count-in when you need one.

Song and section pads

Every section of the song is a pad: Intro, Verse, Chorus. Loop a section while someone speaks or the band vamps.

Mixer, transport and more

Volume, mute and solo for every track, Play, Stop, Rec, click, tempo, Session clips and your plug-in controls.

Works without internet

Your iPad talks straight to your computer over the venue's WiFi or your own router. If the connection drops, it reconnects on its own.

How it works

Ready in five minutes

Nothing to install on the iPad: the app opens in Safari and you add it to your home screen.

Install on your computer

Download the installer and open it. It adds DAW Remote to Ableton Live for you.

Turn it on in Live

In Live's preferences, pick DAWRemote as a control surface.

Scan the QR code

With your iPad or phone on the same WiFi, scan the code and start playing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet at the venue?

No. Your iPad talks straight to your computer over WiFi. You only need internet to activate your license; after that it works for 30 days offline and renews itself whenever your iPad is back online.

What happens if my license expires during a show?

Nothing. DAW Remote never stops in the middle of a session: the change takes effect the next time you open Live.

How many iPads or phones can I connect?

As many as you like. The license is per computer (2 per license), not per device. Musicians can open the stage view in view-only mode.

Does it work with other DAWs besides Ableton Live?

For now it's for Ableton Live. We're working on versions for other DAWs.

Can I move my license to another computer?

Yes. Deactivate it in the app (Settings → License) and activate it on the new one.

How do updates work?

From the app, in Settings → Updates. The new version installs the next time you open Live, never during a show.

What if it's not for me?

You get a free 30-day trial. If you've already bought it, you can ask for a refund within 7 days of purchase.
