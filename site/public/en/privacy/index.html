Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

We collect the minimum needed for your license to work. Controller: Oscar Samuel Ramírez Martínez (RS Software Solutions), Huehuetenango, Guatemala · soporte@dawremote.app.

What we store

  • Your email, when you start the free trial or buy.
  • An identifier for your computer: a hashed fingerprint, not the real serial number, plus the computer's name and operating system. It tells us which computers your license is active on.
  • Your license key and plan, and the dates of activation and of the last check.
  • Server logs, such as the IP address of each request, to prevent abuse. They are deleted regularly.

What we don't store

  • Your setlists, songs, lyrics, pads or anything in your Live projects: all of that stays on your computer and your devices, on your local network.
  • Your card details: payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Tracking or advertising cookies. This site uses no third-party analytics.

What we use it for

To activate and check your license, to email you about it (for example, when your trial is about to end) and to support you. We don't sell or share your data with anyone except the providers we need to run the service (Lemon Squeezy for payments and Hostinger for the server).

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to soporte@dawremote.app. If you delete your data, your license can no longer be verified.

How long

We keep license data while your account exists and for up to 2 years after your license was last checked, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
