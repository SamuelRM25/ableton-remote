Refund policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Before buying you get a free 30-day trial with every feature, so you can use it at rehearsals and shows without paying anything.

7 days to ask for a refund

If you buy and it's not for you, we'll give you a full refund if you ask within 7 days of purchase.

  • Lifetime and yearly: full refund within 7 days of payment.
  • Monthly: the first payment is refundable within 7 days. Monthly renewals are not refunded; you can cancel at any time to stop future charges, and the license stays active until the end of the month already paid.

How to ask

Email soporte@dawremote.app with the email you bought with and the order number from your receipt. Lemon Squeezy refunds the same payment method; it can take 5 to 10 business days to show up, depending on your bank.

What happens to the license

When refunded, the license is deactivated on every computer. It never cuts out during a Live session: it stops working the next time you open Live.
