Welcome to DAW Remote!

Four steps and you're playing.

  1. Open Ableton Live

    If it was already open, quit and reopen it so it loads DAW Remote.

  2. Turn on DAW Remote in Live

    Live → Settings (⌘ ,) → Link, Tempo & MIDI. In a free Control Surface slot choose DAWRemote. Leave Input and Output set to None.

  3. Open the app on this computer

    Go to http://localhost:39031. Start your free 30-day trial or activate your license there.

  4. Connect your iPad or phone

    In the app, go to Settings → Connect another device and scan the QR code with the camera (on the same WiFi). In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to keep it like an app.

Something not working?

  • If DAWRemote isn't in Live's list, quit Live completely and open it again.
  • If the iPad doesn't load, make sure it's on the same WiFi as the computer (not a guest network).
  • Email us at soporte@dawremote.app and we'll help.
